Understand

What is an advance directive?

An advance directive is a legal document that may let someone name a healthcare decision-maker and express treatment preferences for situations when they cannot make or communicate medical decisions.

Requirements vary by state. The person receiving care should guide these decisions while able to do so.

Family Care Village

Who does what?

Healthcare agent: The person legally appointed to make covered healthcare decisions when authorized.

Family Care Village: Relatives and other helpers who may assist with scheduling, transportation, communication, and document coordination. Participation alone does not grant legal decision-making authority.
